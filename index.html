Norms for success: a starter guide for new GiveWhales

James Bedford, Chief of Staff, Malaria

I'm James, the chief of staff for Malaria at GiveWell. I'm writing this eight weeks after I was hired, and I'm still learning GiveWell's culture. This is a list of practices that help me get the best results from the team, especially around Slack and messaging, that I don't think are necessarily natural or customary for everyone coming to GiveWell.

I thought a reference guide to how I actually work might be useful. A caveat: I don't always live up to these ideals myself, and you'll see me break them. These are my suggestions, not rules, and other GiveWhales may reasonably disagree.

How to use this

  • Every norm is numbered, so you can react by number ("yes to 1 and 4, 3 needs a call").
  • The reason matters more than the rule. If a norm doesn't serve its reason in a given case, use judgment.
  • Start with the first seven, on Slack and messaging. Norms 8 to 28 are extra suggestions.
